Frequently asked

Quick answers.

Where do I sign up?

Sign up on the Pixel Forge AI Hackathon Devpost page.

Open Devpost
Do I need to be an AI expert?

No. The hackathon is open to all skill levels. Your project can be simple or advanced as long as AI is meaningfully integrated.

What counts as an AI project?

AI agents, chatbots, automation systems, recommendation engines, AI workflows, creative AI experiences, and other meaningful AI-powered tools all fit the challenge.

Does my project need to be open source?

Yes. Every submission must include a public repository with a valid open-source license file.

Is there a Discord?

Yes. Join the Pixel Forge Discord to connect with the community and get updates.
