2026 Theme · The challenge

Engineer a solution that tackles a real-world challenge.

Build something that solves a problem related to work, personal life, hobbies, productivity, education, creativity, or everyday routines. We want to see how AI can meaningfully improve real experiences and solve practical problems.

Your project can range from a simple chatbot or assistant to a fully autonomous AI agent. What matters most is impact, creativity, and usefulness.

Think beyond prototypes: build something functional that demonstrates how AI can help people in the real world.
